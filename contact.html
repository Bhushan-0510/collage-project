Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get access to ARMS?

All enrolled MCM students receive ARMS login credentials from the department office. Faculty and admin accounts are created by the Head of Department.

What if I forget my ARMS password?

Contact the department office during working hours or email mcm@lrtcommerce.edu with your student ID to request a password reset.

Can I access ARMS from my mobile device?

Yes, ARMS is accessible from any device with internet connectivity including smartphones, tablets, and computers.

How often are resources updated on ARMS?

Faculty members regularly upload new materials and updates. All content is verified by the Head of Department before becoming available to students.
