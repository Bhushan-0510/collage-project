Academic Program

M.Com Computer Management

A comprehensive two-year postgraduate program combining commerce principles with advanced computer applications

The M.Com in Computer Management (MCM) is designed to equip students with both commerce knowledge and technical computer skills. The program spans four semesters with a curriculum that progressively builds expertise in areas such as database management, web technologies, advanced computing, and research methodology.

Detailed syllabus documents, notes, and study materials for each semester are available through ARMS after login.

Course Breakdown

Semester-wise Structure

1

Semester 1 – Computer Applications Fundamentals

Introduction to computer applications, programming fundamentals, office automation, and basic database concepts. Foundation semester establishing core technical skills.

2

Semester 2 – Database & Web Technologies

Advanced database management systems, SQL programming, web development (HTML, CSS, JavaScript), and introduction to server-side scripting.

3

Semester 3 – Advanced Computing & E-Commerce

Advanced programming, software engineering, e-commerce applications, digital marketing, and enterprise resource planning systems.

4

Semester 4 – Project & Research Methodology

Research methodology, project work, emerging technologies, data analytics, and comprehensive practical application of learned concepts.
